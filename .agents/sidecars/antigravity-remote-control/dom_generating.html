Okay! I took a step back, captured a screenshot of the desktop app during an active generation, and meticulously analyzed the DOM in both states. Here is what was going wrong:

The Cancel (Ctrl+D) button on the desktop app is actually always present in the DOM (it sits right next to the microphone button, even when idle, to allow canceling active inputs). Because my previous fix relied on simply checking if that button existed, the phone app thought the desktop app was always generating, causing the Stop button to remain stuck on your screen forever!

The Fix: I discovered that when the desktop app is truly generating a response, it dynamically injects a tiny pulsing indicator at the bottom with a specific attribute: data-testid="agent-loading". I have updated the isGenerating logic in server.js to look for this specific loading indicator instead of the Cancel button. The /stop logic itself remains the same (it still correctly clicks the desktop's Cancel button to halt the generation).

I've restarted the server with this fix. The Stop button should now flawlessly appear when the AI is typing, and successfully disappear the moment it stops or when you cancel it! Try sending another message to test it out.
