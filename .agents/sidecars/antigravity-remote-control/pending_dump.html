Because Tailscale is an operating system-level VPN, the solution depends on which side of the connection you're talking about (your Windows PC vs. your Phone).

We can't write code in the web app to "auto-launch" Tailscale on your phone, because if Tailscale is down, your phone can't reach the server to load the web app in the first place! It's a chicken-and-egg problem.

However, you can configure both devices so that Tailscale is strictly Always-On or On-Demand:

1. On Your Phone (iOS / Android)

You can use Tailscale's native On-Demand feature to automatically spin up the VPN the second you try to connect to the app, without you having to open the Tailscale app manually.

  • iOS: Open the Tailscale app, tap your avatar (top right), go to Settings > On-Demand. Enable it, and set it so that whenever you are on Cellular or an untrusted Wi-Fi, the VPN automatically connects.
  • Android: Go to your Android system settings > Network & internet > VPN. Tap the gear icon next to Tailscale and toggle on Always-on VPN.

2. On Your Windows PC (The Server)

By default, Tailscale on Windows installs a background service (Tailscale service) that starts automatically when your PC boots, even before you log in.

  • If you've been manually launching the desktop app, you don't need to. As long as the Tailscale app is set to "Start with Windows" in its preferences (or via the Task Manager Startup tab), your computer will always be sitting on the Tailscale network ready to receive your phone's connection.

If you set your phone to "On-Demand" and your PC to "Start with Windows", you will never have to think about the VPN again. You just tap the app icon on your phone, Tailscale silently connects in the background within milliseconds, and the app loads perfectly securely over the private tunnel!
