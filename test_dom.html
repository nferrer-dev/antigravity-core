I believe I found the root cause of the thumbs up/down icons highlighting but not actually clicking!

The Issue: When the phone taps an element, the server first calls target.focus() on the desktop element to prepare it. In the desktop UI, focusing the thumbs up/down buttons causes a Tooltip (e.g. "Good response") to immediately pop up and render perfectly over the center of the button. Because our remote-click proxy dispatches a true, hardware-level mouse click to the exact center of the element, the click was being intercepted by the Tooltip overlay instead of hitting the button underneath!

The Fix: I modified server.js to dispatch a direct programmatic .click() on the target DOM node (and its closest <button> wrapper, just in case you tap the SVG icon inside) right before it attempts the hardware click. Programmatic clicks operate at the DOM level and ignore visual overlap, which perfectly bypasses the tooltip interference!
